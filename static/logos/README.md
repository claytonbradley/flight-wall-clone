# Airline logo assets

The flight display prefers standalone airline symbols. When an airline does not publish a distinct symbol, it uses a vetted corporate logo or wordmark. The ICAO badge is retained only when neither type of trustworthy asset is available.

Envoy Air (`ENY`) uses the American flight symbol because it operates American Eagle flights. CommuteAir (`UCA`) uses the United globe because it operates United Express flights. These aliases avoid displaying the operators' text-only wordmarks.

Except for the United, GoJet, and Mesa sources, the source pages and licensing information are on Wikimedia Commons at `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:<source file>`. The United source file is on English Wikipedia. The GoJet and Mesa marks come from their respective official airline websites.

Automatic downloads prefer Wikimedia candidates described as a symbol, icon, emblem, mark, crane, heart, shield, or tail. If none is available, a matching logo or wordmark is accepted. Every accepted download records its `asset_kind` in `logo-cache/sources.json`.

`PDT.png` is Piedmont Airlines' current horizontal Speedbird wordmark, sourced from the airline's official website after its 2026 brand refresh: https://piedmont-airlines.com/

Airline names and symbols may be protected trademarks. Their presence identifies tracked aircraft and does not imply endorsement or affiliation.
